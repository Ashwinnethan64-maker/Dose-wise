# Overview

DoseWise AI is an AI-powered medication management platform designed to help users organize medications, receive reminders, understand medicine information, and improve medication adherence through an intuitive web application.

The platform combines medication management with artificial intelligence to simplify healthcare tracking while providing a modern and user-friendly experience.

---

# Problem Statement

Many individuals struggle to:

- Remember medication schedules
- Manage multiple prescriptions
- Understand medicine usage
- Track medication history
- Access reliable medicine information quickly

DoseWise AI addresses these challenges by providing a centralized platform powered by AI assistance.
